# THE CIRCUIT

*A premium documentary series*

---

## LOGLINE

Inside the billion-dollar world of international show jumping — where Olympic athletes, dynastic heirs, and the horses that bind them chase the sport's ultimate prize across a year on the road to the 2028 Los Angeles Games.

---

## ONE-PAGER

There is a world most people have never seen.

It moves in a circle. Wellington in winter. Aachen in summer. The Hamptons in late August. Geneva by Christmas. The same eighty or so faces — the riders, the owners, the grooms, the families — show up at each stop, set up their barns, compete for millions, and leave for the next city. They live their entire lives inside this orbit. They call it **the circuit**.

It is one of the wealthiest professional sports on earth, and one of the least televised. A single horse can sell for fifteen million dollars. A season campaigning at the top level costs more than a Premier League salary. The grooms who care for these animals know them better than any human in their lives. The riders are athletes, but they are also showmen, businesspeople, and in many cases, the children of the most powerful families in the world. Bruce Springsteen's daughter is on the U.S. Olympic team. Bill Gates' daughter married an Egyptian Olympian. Steve Jobs' daughter is a working international rider. They compete against the working professionals who built their careers from nothing — McLain Ward, Kent Farrington, Laura Kraut — riders who have spent thirty years getting to where the heirs arrived as teenagers.

In 2028, the Olympics come home. For the first time since 1984, the equestrian Games will be held on American soil, at Galway Downs in Southern California. American show jumping has not produced an individual Olympic gold medalist since Joe Fargis won in Los Angeles forty-four years earlier. Every rider on the circuit — American, European, Middle Eastern, South American — is now building toward one stadium and a single weekend in July 2028.

***The Circuit*** is a two-season premium documentary series that follows that journey. Season one, filmed across 2027, builds the world: the year-long tour, the rivalries, the bloodlines, the partnerships between human and horse that define the sport. Season two, filmed across 2028, follows the same characters as their orbit narrows toward Los Angeles — through team selection, qualification trials, late-breaking injuries, and finally the Olympic stadium itself.

From the team behind premium sports documentary, *The Circuit* treats show jumping the way *Drive to Survive* treated Formula 1 and *Full Swing* treated the PGA Tour — not as a niche curiosity, but as the front door to a world the audience never realized was waiting for them. A world of generational wealth, generational athletes, and the four-legged partners who carry both.

The horse, after all, is the only Olympic athlete that doesn't know it's competing. That, more than anything, is what makes this sport cinema.

---

## WHY THIS SERIES, WHY NOW

**Documentary sports has become prestige television.** *Drive to Survive*, *Full Swing*, *Break Point*, *Race for the Crown*, *Quarterback*, *Beckham*, *Tour de France: Unchained* — the genre has matured into the most reliable subscriber-driver in streaming. But show jumping — the most photogenic, most globally distributed, most narratively rich of all horse sports — has never been given the premium-doc treatment.

**The sport has a built-in season finale.** The 2028 Los Angeles Olympics is the most marketable American Olympics in a generation. A two-season series that climaxes there is a buyer's dream: a clean structure, a fixed delivery date, and a guaranteed cultural moment to release into.

**The cast is already famous.** *The Circuit* doesn't need to manufacture stars. Springsteen, Jobs, Gates, Bloomberg, Selleck, Cuoco — the children of the people who built modern America are already in this world. So are the working-class kids from Texas and Chicago who out-rode them. The collision is the show.

**The visual language is unmatched.** Wellington palm trees at golden hour. The grass arena at Aachen. Snow falling outside Geneva. Slim Aarons compositions with Sebastião Salgado humanity. Warm, clean, colorful, luxurious. The footage sells itself.

**No one else has the access.** This is a world that runs on relationships, generational trust, and quiet introductions. Whoever gets in first owns this story.

## FORMAT

Eight episodes per season. Approximately 50 minutes each. Premium documentary in the *Drive to Survive* / *Full Swing* tradition: observational, character-led, structured around the real-world calendar of the sport. Each episode anchored to a major event on the circuit, each opening up a different storyline within the larger ensemble.

---

## SEASON ONE — 2027 — "THE CIRCUIT"

Eight episodes following the year on the road. Winter Equestrian Festival in Wellington. The European spring through Rome and the Dutch Masters. CHIO Aachen. The Hampton Classic. Spruce Meadows. CHI Geneva. The audience meets the cast, learns the world, and watches the rivalries take shape — with the Olympic year now visible on the horizon.

## SEASON TWO — 2028 — "ROAD TO LOS ANGELES"

Eight episodes. Same characters, narrowing focus. Team selection trials. Horse-and-rider partnerships under pressure. Injuries, withdrawals, last-minute substitutions. The finale episode is the 2028 Los Angeles Olympic Games, held at Galway Downs, California — the first home Olympics for American equestrians in forty-four years.

---

## CREATIVE PRINCIPLES

**Horses are characters, not props.** This series will give the animals the same narrative weight as the humans. The audience should know the horses by name — Ilex, Greya, Baloutinue, Argan de Beliard — and feel their careers, injuries, and partnerships as deeply as the riders'.

**Wealth is shown, not narrated.** The world of *The Circuit* speaks for itself. No graphics explaining the cost of a horse. The camera does the work.

**The sport, explained through people.** The audience does not need to know what a jump-off is on episode one. They need to know who is in it, and what it means to them.
